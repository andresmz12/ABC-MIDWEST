How It Works

From first punch-in to payday

WorkTrack handles the whole cycle automatically — no paperwork, no manual tracking, no spreadsheets.

1

Employee clocks in

From any phone — tap Clock In, snap a photo. Time, GPS address, and photo evidence captured instantly. No app install needed.

2

Manager reviews live

The dashboard shows who's working, where, and for how long. Verify punch-in addresses on an interactive map. Flag discrepancies instantly.

3

Export & pay your team

One click generates a full payroll report per employee. Export to Excel and pay your whole team in minutes — not hours.
