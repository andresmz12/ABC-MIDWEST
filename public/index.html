How It Works

From first punch-in to payday

WorkTrack handles the whole cycle automatically — no paperwork, no manual tracking.

1

Employee clocks in

From any phone — tap Clock In, snap a photo. Time, GPS address, and evidence captured instantly.

2

Manager reviews in real time

The dashboard shows who's working, where, and how long. Review shifts, approve time cards, verify addresses on the map.

3

Export & pay your team

One click generates a full payroll report by employee. Export to Excel and pay your team in minutes, not hours.

Features

Everything your operation needs

Built for cleaning companies. Simple for employees, powerful for managers.

Clock In / Clock Out

Employees clock in from any smartphone with a photo. Timestamps are automatic and tamper-proof.

GPS Address Tracking

See exactly where employees clocked in and out on an interactive map. Download a full address report to verify every shift.

Payroll Reports

Automatic hour summaries per employee and location. Export to Excel in one click — payroll in minutes, not hours.

Team Management

Add employees, assign locations, set hourly rates. Manage permissions and see who's active right now.

Scheduling & Calendar

Schedule jobs by location, assign employees, and let them see their upcoming calendar from their phone.

Client Invoicing

Generate professional invoices for your clients, track payment status, and export PDF receipts — all in one place.
